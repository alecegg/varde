## Hierarchy moves
- Page title, one-line reason, then the fields. Remove everything that is not needed to finish.
- Required marking: mark the rare case (optional) rather than every field.
- Field text 16 px or more on mobile, targets 44 px high, the focus ring clear on every field.
- Help text goes under the field in muted but readable colour; errors replace it in the same slot.

## Before and after
Before: a two-column form with placeholder-only labels, a grey disabled "Submit", and a red banner reading "Error". After: one column, visible labels, an enabled "Create account", and an error under the email field reading "That address is already registered. Sign in instead."
