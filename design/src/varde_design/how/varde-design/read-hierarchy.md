## Hierarchy moves
- One h1, h2 sections, h3 subsections; no skipped levels. Heading space above is about twice the space below, so each heading binds to what follows.
- Body 16-18 px, line height 1.5-1.6, paragraph gap about one line. Headings differ from body by size and weight, steps of about 1.25.
- Links are underlined or clearly coloured and visible in running text; visited state is optional.
- Pull quotes and callouts at most one per screen; they interrupt, so earn them.
- Use `text-wrap: balance` on headings and `pretty` on paragraphs where supported.

## Before and after
Before: full-width text at 1440 px with three card callouts in a row and identical heading sizes. After: a 68-character column, h2 clearly above h3, one callout, a right-hand contents list; the eye follows the column down.
