## Layout recipes
- First viewport at 390 and 1440 px: a short name or logo bar and the headline stating the offer. It also holds one supporting sentence, one primary action, and the proof or product view. See craft.md for hero compositions and ratios.
- Section order: offer, proof (real numbers, names or product views), how it works in three or fewer steps, details, repeated action, quiet footer. Skip sections you have no real material for.
- Every section uses the full width set by the hero. When text takes one side, the other holds a figure, list, number or quote, never nothing.
- Pricing or choices: at most three options, the recommended one marked with a label and a filled button; others outlined.
- Do not repeat the same layout three times in a row; vary which side holds the text.
