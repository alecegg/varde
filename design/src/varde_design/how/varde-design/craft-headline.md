## Headline arithmetic
- Characters per line is about line width in px divided by (font size times 0.5). Check the headline at 390 px before choosing words.
- A 350 px line at 34 px holds about 20 characters; a 1100 px line at 104 px holds about 21. Plan for 32 characters or fewer, four to six words, to stay within two lines at every width.
- A long intro sentence or quotation set large is a statement. Mark it up as a paragraph at 36 px or less, not as a heading. Cut words first, widen the measure second, shrink the size last.

## Build and fix order
- Build order: hero first, then one inspect, one fix batch, one confirm. Fix order: idea, hierarchy, composition, type, colour, detail, motion.
