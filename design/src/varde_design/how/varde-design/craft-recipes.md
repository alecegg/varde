## Rests versus holes
- Holes sit beside a short column, under a removed decoration, or in the half of a split section that holds nothing. Fix by adding the figure, list or number the section needs, or by changing the split.
- Outer margins are equal left and right at every width; count left text edges down the page: two or three reused on purpose, not six.

## Hero and composition recipes
- Split: copy 5 of 12 columns, figure 7 of 12, copy vertically centred on the figure. Stack below 800 px with copy first, figure second, both at full width.
- Centred: headline over 8 of 12 columns, one sentence over 6, one action; works only when proof sits directly under it.
- Full-bleed figure: copy block over the quiet third of the image, dark or light overlay chosen for 4.5:1 contrast. The copy block never covers the subject.
- Product view: crop to the 60 percent of the product that carries the idea, large enough to read its text at 1440 px.
- Stacking rule: headline, support sentence, action, proof, with 24-32 px between the first three and 48-64 px before the proof. Navigation stays one row.
- The first viewport shows a hint of the next section so the page reads as scrollable.
