## Layout recipes
- Single column, 360-480 px wide, centred or left-aligned in the content area; one field per row except short pairs (city and postcode, month and year).
- Labels sit above fields, always visible; placeholders show format examples only. Group related fields under a short heading with 24-32 px above it and 12-16 px between fields.
- Multi-step: a step indicator ("Step 2 of 4" plus step names), one decision per step, back and next in a fixed place. The summary of earlier answers stays reachable.
- Settings: sections with their own save, or autosave with a visible "Saved" message; never mix the two.
- Footer row: one primary submit with a verb label ("Create account"), a quiet secondary ("Cancel"). On mobile make the primary full width.
