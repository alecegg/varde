## Hierarchy moves
- Headline 40-72 px on desktop, 32-40 px on mobile, two lines at most; body 18-20 px; section headings 28-36 px.
- One filled primary action per viewport. A secondary link is text, not a second filled button.
- Gaps between sections (80-120 px desktop, 56-72 px mobile) clearly larger than gaps inside sections.
- Proof beats claims: a specific number, a named customer, or a view of the product replaces adjectives.

## Before and after
Before: a centred headline "Revolutionize your workflow" over a gradient, three icon cards, and two competing buttons. After: a left-aligned 5-word headline naming the product's job, one sentence, one "Start free" button, and a real product screenshot right. The squint test shows headline, button, image in that order.
