## Hierarchy moves
- Title 20-24 px, section label 14-16 px, row text 14-16 px, meta 12-13 px (never below the S4 minimum). Contrast comes from weight and colour, not from many sizes.
- The primary action is the only filled button on screen; secondary actions are outlined or text.
- Status uses an icon or word plus colour, never colour alone.
- Group with space (16 px inside a group, 32 px or more between groups) before adding borders or cards.

## Before and after
Before: three equal cards of big numbers above a table, a blue button in every row, the title floating in empty space. After: a title row with one "New order" button, a one-line stat strip, then the table with row actions folded into a menu. The eye lands on the button, then the first row.
