## Layout recipes
- Single reading column 60-75 characters wide (about 34-42 rem at 16-18 px), centred or beside a left nav; do not stretch text across 1440 px.
- Docs: left nav 220-260 px, article column, optional right "on this page" list at 180-220 px that hides below 1100 px.
- Long reports: lead block (title, one-sentence summary, date, author) then sections; a short contents list when over four sections.
- Figures and tables may break out wider than the text column, never narrower than the text at 390 px. Wrap tables in a scroll container so the page does not overflow.
- Code blocks: scroll horizontally inside the block, 14 px or more, distinct background.
