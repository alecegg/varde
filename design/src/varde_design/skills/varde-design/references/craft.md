# Craft: rests, heroes, headlines

Applies to a first screen or a marketing-style section, alongside the page-type reference.

- At most one rest (empty space placed on purpose) per page. Every empty region is closed by a margin, a gap between groups, or a figure. One you cannot name is a hole, and it gets filled.
- A screen that is mostly empty ground is underbuilt, not restrained. Fill the view at readable size (16 px body, full-contrast ink) before adding space.
- Outer margins are equal left and right at every width.
- The headline stays within two lines at 390 px and at 1440 px, in 32 characters or fewer.
- A long intro sentence or quotation set large is marked up as a paragraph at 36 px or less, not as a heading.
- Copy over a full-bleed image keeps 4.5:1 contrast and never covers the subject.
- The first viewport shows a hint of the next section so the page reads as scrollable.
