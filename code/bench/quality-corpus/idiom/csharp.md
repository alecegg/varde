## Idiomatic patterns

C# commonly uses properties, interfaces, dependency injection, partial classes, LINQ, and asynchronous methods with `async` and `await`. Frameworks often supply conventional entry points and registration patterns.

## Not a defect by itself

An auto-property, a LINQ chain, an asynchronous continuation, or framework-shaped structure is not a defect by itself. Consider whether the operation and its effects remain easy to follow.
