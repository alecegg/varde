topic: varde-improve/improve-steps

summary: Ordered steps for the timer-started friction improvement proposal
---

1. Run `varde learn friction list --status open --json`; inspect items with
   `friction show <ID>`.
2. Run `varde learn friction recurrence --json`, then inspect historical reports.
3. Select a group only when at least two real occurrences share an obstacle and
   improvement target. Verify their evidence and inspect the current source and
   guidance.
4. Draft a plan or skill-edit proposal that holds:
   - source IDs and exact target;
   - impact, risks, and items to promote;
   - a removal option;
   - the four audit answers from `references/options.md`;
   - one outcome tag.

5. Present the proposal and stop for human approval before editing files or
   recording adoption.
