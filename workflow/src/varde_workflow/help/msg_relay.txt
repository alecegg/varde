Run the inbox relay once or wait for due work

Usage: varde msg relay (--once | --wait) --as <MAILMAN_TOKEN> [--timeout <SECONDS>] [--now <TIMESTAMP>] [--context-tokens N]
       varde msg relay confirm <ACTION_ID> --result <RESULT> [--evidence <TEXT>] [--by <MAILMAN_TOKEN>] [--harness <NAME>] [--now <TIMESTAMP>]
       varde msg relay renew <ACTION_ID> [--now <TIMESTAMP>]
       varde msg relay end --as <MAILMAN_TOKEN> [--now <TIMESTAMP>]

The relay writes one varde-relay/1 JSON object to stdout. Diagnostics go to stderr.
Exit codes: 0 success, 64 invalid arguments or mailman token, 70 internal error.
Wait defaults to a 540-second timeout. Results have status `actions`, `none` (at timeout), or `superseded`.
Due urgent messages may be escalated to `main` when unbound or after repeated failures.
