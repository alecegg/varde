Report delivery status and inbox lag

Usage: varde msg status [--json] [--now <TIMESTAMP>]

Options:
      --json          Print the report as JSON
      --now <TIMESTAMP>  Use an ISO 8601 timestamp for age calculations
  -h, --help          Print help

Exit status:
  0   No current alert
  1   Overdue unread mail, urgent mail due without a live mailman, or a
      suspect, misdelivered, token-reuse, or supersede event in the last 24 h
  70  Status report could not be loaded

Mailman state is alive, dead, or none. Per-mailman output includes its last
heartbeat or return, expected return time, delivery count, and recreate flag.
Suspect and misdelivered counts are included. The stopped-reading line is
informational: pending mail is over 10 minutes old, the binding has not been
seen for over 30 minutes, and there has been no push or leased action in that
period.
