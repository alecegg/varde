Audit one source commit against its task ownership

Usage: varde plan check-task-ownership [OPTIONS] --task <TASK> --commit <COMMIT>

Options:
      --task <TASK>                 
      --commit <COMMIT>             
      --repo-root <REPO_ROOT>       [default: .]
      --trusted-tip <TRUSTED_TIPS>  Integration commit already merged into the worker (repeatable)
      --json                        
  -h, --help                        Print help
