Record a main-agent nudge without sending a message

Usage: varde msg nudge --to <SLOT> --reason <REASON>

Options:
      --to <SLOT>       Agent slot being nudged
      --reason <REASON> idle-unread, stalled, or other
  -h, --help            Print help

Appends a main-nudge record to the inbox nudges.jsonl log. It sends nothing.
