Set or clear the harness-neutral owner for one plan

Usage: varde plan owner [OPTIONS] <PLAN> [HARNESS:ID]

Arguments:
  <PLAN>        Plan path or identifier
  [HARNESS:ID]  Owner identifier in opaque `<harness>:<id>` form

Options:
      --clear  Remove the plan owner
  -h, --help   Print help (see more with '--help')
