Verify the review report and mark a plan completed

Usage: varde plan conclude [OPTIONS] <PLAN>

Arguments:
  <PLAN>  

Options:
      --merged <MERGED>  Commit the reviewed work is merged as (defaults to the repository HEAD)
      --repo <REPO>      Repository holding the reviewed and merged commits
      --json             
  -h, --help             Print help (see more with '--help')
