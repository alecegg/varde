Record an answer to an open question

Usage: varde plan answer [OPTIONS] <PLAN> --question <TEXT-OR-INDEX> --answer <TEXT>

Arguments:
  <PLAN>
          Plan path or identifier

Options:
      --question <TEXT-OR-INDEX>
          Exact open question bullet text or its 1-based position

      --answer <TEXT>
          Answer to record (newlines are collapsed to one line)

      --json
          Print a JSON result envelope

  -h, --help
          Print help
