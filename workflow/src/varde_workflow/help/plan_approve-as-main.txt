Record a checked merge approval as main

Usage: varde plan approve-as-main <PLAN> --note <TEXT> --plan-followed --gate-result pass --abnormal-events <TEXT>

Reads the runner's merge-approver-main secret and posts a checklist tied to reviewed_commit. Requires a passing review.md verdict and explicit checklist options.

Arguments:
  <PLAN>        Plan path or identifier
  --note <TEXT>           Short approval note (required)
  --plan-followed         Confirm the plan was followed (required)
  --gate-result <TEXT>    Gate result; must be pass (required)
  --abnormal-events TEXT  Record abnormal events or none (required)

Options:
  -h, --help    Print help
